      ## Rollout update and rollback


      Rollout update strategies: 1) Rolling Update: Launch before terminate  {max surge}  -  {Default strategy}
                                 2) Recreate : terminate before launch   {max unavailability}
                                 3) Canary Deployment: deployment in parts
                                 4) Blue-green Deployment: zero downtime but costliest deployment

  name: my-pvc

    team: dev

  accessModes:
    - ReadWriteOnce
  volumeMode: Filesystem
  resources:
    requests:
      storage: 500Mi
  volumeName: my-pv
  storageClassName: manual
